2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect personal data in the following categories:

2.1 Data You Provide Directly

• Full name and username

Eventsphere Ltd uses your personal data for the following purposes:

3.1 To Provide and Improve the Platform

• Creating and managing your attendee account
• Delivering real-time, location-based event recommendations tailored to your preferences
• Processing your event interactions including RSVPs, check-ins, and reactions
• Providing customer support and responding to your enquiries
• Diagnosing and fixing technical issues

3.2 For Marketing & Communications

• Sending you event recommendations, platform updates, and promotional notifications
• Serving you personalized in-app content and branded event promotions

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time through your account settings or by contacting us at support@wyakenya.com. Opting out of marketing messages will not affect essential service communications.

3.3 For Safety, Legal & Compliance Purposes

• Enforcing our Attendee Terms & Conditions and other platform policies
• Detecting, investigating, and preventing fraud, abuse, or illegal activity
• Complying with our obligations under applicable Kenyan law
• Responding to lawful requests from law enforcement or regulatory authorities

3.4 For Analytics & Platform Development

• Generating anonymized and aggregated usage insights to understand how the Platform is used
• Improving algorithm performance for event discovery and content personalization
• Informing product decisions and future feature development

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

• Consent — where you have given us explicit consent, such as for location data and marketing communications.
• Contractual necessity — where processing is necessary to perform our contract with you (i.e., to provide the WYA Platform).
• Legal obligation — where processing is required to comply with applicable Kenyan law.
• Legitimate interests — where processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests, such as fraud prevention and platform analytics, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights.

You have the right to withdraw consent at any time where consent is the basis for processing. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

5. Sharing of User Content with Event Organizers

This section is important and we ask that you read it carefully.

When you post content on the WYA Platform — including but not limited to event check-ins, photos, videos, captions, reactions, and reviews relating to a specific event — you acknowledge and agree that:

• Such User-Generated Content may be shared with the relevant Event Organizer(s) responsible for that event for the purpose of event promotion and marketing.
• Event Organizers may use your Content across their own promotional channels, including social media platforms, websites, email campaigns, printed materials, and event recaps, subject to the terms they have agreed with Eventsphere Ltd.
• Your username or display name may be associated with your Content when it is shared with Organizers.
• Eventsphere Ltd does not sell your personal data to Event Organizers. The sharing of Content is limited strictly to the promotional purposes described in this section.

If you do not wish your content to be shared with Event Organizers for promotional purposes, you may:

• Adjust your content-sharing preferences in your account privacy settings (where available), or
• Refrain from posting content relating to specific events on the Platform, or
• Contact us at support@wyakenya.com to request that specific content not be shared.

Eventsphere Ltd will endeavor to ensure content attribution is maintained where possible. However, once content has been shared with an Event Organizer, we cannot guarantee how that Organizer stores or further uses it. We encourage you to review the privacy practices of Event Organizers whose events you engage with on the Platform.

9. Your Rights Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019

As a data subject under the KDPA, you have the following rights in respect of your personal data:

• Right of Access — to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
• Right to Rectification — to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
• Right to Erasure — to request deletion of your personal data where it is no longer necessary for the purposes it was collected.
• Right to Object — to object to the processing of your personal data for direct marketing or where processing is based on our legitimate interests.
• Right to Restriction — to request that we restrict processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.
• Right to Data Portability — to receive a copy of your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
• Right to Withdraw Consent — to withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@wyakenya.com. We will respond to your request within 21 days as required by the KDPA. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request. If you are unsatisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) of Kenya.
